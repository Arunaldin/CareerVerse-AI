The STAR Framework for HR Rounds:

S - Situation Set the context and describe the challenge or project.
T - Task Explain your specific responsibility in that scenario.
A - Action Detail the exact steps and technologies you employed.
R - Result Quantify the positive outcome, metrics, and lessons learned.

1. "Tell me about yourself."

Strategy: Use the Past-Present-Future formula: Background -> Key college achievements & projects -> Why you're excited about this specific company.

2. "What are your greatest strengths and weaknesses?"

Strategy: Back your strength with a real project example. Pick a genuine weakness you are actively improving (e.g., public speaking, over-committing) and state the exact steps you are taking to mitigate it.

3. "Why should we hire you over other candidates?"

Strategy: Align your proven quick-learning agility, strong CS fundamentals, and cultural alignment with the team's ongoing mission.
